The python script ERTM2160.py is a adaptation or translation of matlab script "ertm2160_2013_v2.m" (Christian
Hirt, WA Centre for Geodesy, Curtin University)

The links for the original matlab script and the ERTM2160.py adaptation are:
https://ddfe.curtin.edu.au/gravitymodels/ERTM2160/software/ertm2160_2013_v2.m

the links for the download the ERTM2160 model are:
https://ddfe.curtin.edu.au/gravitymodels/ERTM2160/
https://ddfe.blazejbucha.com/models/
